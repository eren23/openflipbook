# Ankh-Morpork: Spatially Anchored Demo Map

Revised September 10, 2026. This is a deliberate fan-map layout based on the
provided brief, not a claim to reproduce the official Discworld street plan.
No image generation or paid model call accompanies this document.

## What Changed

- The city starts below the title and motto. Dolly Sisters and Nap Hill now sit
  inside the wall rather than beneath the title or outside the enclosure.
- The university extends down to the actual riverbank. Its garden, jetty, gate,
  Sator Square, and Brass Bridge have connected approaches.
- River decoration no longer competes with the bridge label. The bridge statues
  have four fixed positions outside the walking lane.
- The five city gates and two water passages are separate, explicit openings.
  Shambling Gate is not drawn as an accidental sixth gate.
- Short Street skirts the Shades instead of cutting a labelled thoroughfare
  through its deliberately unlabelled interior.
- Docks and ships stop before the downstream water grille. Ships cannot be
  stranded outside the wall while being described as inside it.
- Road access to Sto Lat bends around the title instead of running through it.
- The Mended Drum has a fixed footprint, entrance, street frontage, service yard,
  and three nearby orientation anchors. Elevations and hidden rooms remain
  separate authoring decisions, not facts supposedly recovered from a roof plan.

## Copy-Ready Image Prompt

Create ONE exceptionally detailed, flat, top-down fantasy city map of
ANKH-MORPORK from Discworld, in a 16:9 landscape composition. Render an antique
hand-inked cartographer's chart, not a painting of a map on a desk. The image is
the chart itself. Use the highest available landscape resolution. Preserve the
specified layout even if small decorative details must be simplified.

PRIORITY ORDER: correct banks and connected streets; unique landmark identities;
clear entrances and bridges; readable major labels; dense architecture;
incidental jokes and ornament. Do not sacrifice the first four for extra detail.

COORDINATES AND DRAWING RULES

Coordinates are percentages of the whole rectangular image: x increases to the
right, y increases downward. They are not metres. At 16:9, equal x/y percentage
distances are not equal physical lengths; circular symbols must still look round.
Bounding boxes below reserve actual ground footprints, not areas in which text
may overlap buildings. All annotations and furniture remain inside a 2% margin.
An inset neatline sits at x=3 and x=97. The river reaches these inner boundaries,
not the physical image edges; its terminal hatching is fully inside the margin.

Draw footprints and roofs in true orthographic plan. Never show upright facades,
tilted buildings, an isometric city, or a horizon. Height is suggested only by
roof structure, contouring, and short consistent cast shadows. Do not draw an
elevation of the Tower of Art in the middle of an otherwise top-down map.

## Demo Staging Contract: Separate From The Image Prompt

The local dimensions preserve the proposed root-map footprint's aspect ratio.
For the brief's 16:9 canvas, choosing a 10 m tavern width gives:
`local_x = 8 + (map_x - 44.6) * (10 / 2.6)` and
`local_z = 13 + (map_y - 52.8) * (10 / 2.6) * (9 / 16)`.
The x/y percentage factors differ because the canvas is rectangular; this is
one uniform physical pixel scale, not anisotropic stretching. Consequently the
tavern's 2.5% image-height depth becomes approximately 5.4 m, not an unrelated
9 m guess. Re-register against the actual rendered map before accepting this
scene: a prompt's coordinate request is not proof that the renderer obeyed it.

The right turn is correct because a south-facing observer's right is west.
The left turn into the doorway is correct because a west-facing observer's
left is south. A video prompt saying merely "move forward into the tavern"
would lose exactly this directional information.

Author and approve facade heights, door/window counts, roof pitch, materials,
and collision geometry before this becomes a demo. A conservative first
proposal is a two-storey tavern, 5.5 m eaves, 7 m ridge, one north-facing door,
and three upper windows. These are design choices, not visible facts from the
top-down chart. No interior shot until a matching interior is actually built.
